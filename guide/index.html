No. Everything in the guide works with your eyes alone. One section shows what a simple pair of binoculars adds.

Does it work where I live?

Yes, in both hemispheres. It includes charts for northern and southern skies. What you see depends on your weather, latitude and light pollution, and the guide shows you how to plan around them.

A 17-page PDF, designed to read on a phone or tablet. Its dark pages are easy on night-adapted eyes, but use a lot of ink if you print them.
